# The apps' icons (`services/<service>/icon.png`)

Each app's card on the portal's dashboard shows its `icon.png`, unless you
put your own in `custom/branding/icons/<service>.png` (`./mediastack.sh brand
app-icon <service> <file.png>`).

The logos are their projects' own, and their trademarks; they are shown to
name each app, which implies no endorsement.

Where they come from (taken 24 Nov 2026; made square on a transparent
background and at most 512 pixels a side, nothing else changed):

- **Dashboard Icons** by Homarr Labs —
  <https://github.com/homarr-labs/dashboard-icons>, Apache License 2.0
  (<https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0>): every icon here but
  WatchState's. `pihole` is their `pi-hole`; `radarr-4k` and `sonarr-anime`
  are their `radarr` and `sonarr`.
- **WatchState** by ArabCoders — its own logo,
  <https://github.com/arabcoders/watchstate> (`frontend/public/images/logo.png`),
  MIT License.

A service with no `icon.png` shows authentik's letter until you give it one.
